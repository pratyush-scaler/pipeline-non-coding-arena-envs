### T3: Quantity basis cued only on or about the record (tax-inclusive, per pack)

- **Status:** proven
- **Family:** quantity_basis
- **Aimed at:** both
- **Clock trap:** no
- **Aliases:** Q.taxincl, Q.pack, quantity basis
- **Pooled misses:** Fable 153/235 (65%), Astra 49/200 (24%)
- **Loopholes:** L-default, L-shield, L-threshold

A price or quantity is read on the wrong basis: a tax-inclusive bill read as net, a price per pair or pack read as per piece. The basis is cued only on or about the record itself (a cheque note, a received-goods tally, the seller's filed orders), never in a caption next to the figures. Fable misses tax-inclusive bases; per-pack pricing with a tally bites both. Measured here (build_evidence.py, 2026-10-15): final 10x2 pooled Fable 78/90, Astra 23/90; all stages Fable 153/235, Astra 49/200 (AMBIGUOUS/DEFECT units excluded). Audited GENUINE in 4 task(s). Status proven: audited GENUINE in 4 tasks; pooled final miss rate >= 0.2 with n >= 20 on fable, astra (unaudited >= 90% units excluded).

**Recipe**

- *record:* a bill, list price or order line whose figures look complete on the default basis
- *anchors:* a cue on the record or about this record (a tally of what was received, the seller's own filed order lines, a remark that the rise was not passed on)
- *withheld:* no caption or column header states the basis
- *dials:*
  - change the kind of evidence (a received-goods tally moved Astra where more filed lists of the same kind did not: cnc 2.6.0 P4)
  - the weakest oblique cue restored the Fable bite on gst P3 ('same rates as our counter board')
- *conservation:* the per-piece reading must contradict a received count or a total
- *shields:* a 'printed rate governs' rule must be scoped to rates, not to what the price includes

**Proof obligations (authoring/validate.py)**

- uniqueness: only one basis reproduces the tally or the paid total
- ablation: without the on-record cue the basis is underdetermined (executed)
- blind reader: a non-governing-document cue that the reader also misses is unfair

**Hazards**

- a cue in a caption next to the figures is a stated rule: suitcase 2.2.0/2.3.0 P7 Fable 0/10
- a cue only in a non-governing document with one out-of-population figure: suitcase 2.4.0/2.5.0 P7, blind reader also missed (UNCONFIRMED)
- a single route is GENUINE-soft: cnc 2.3.0 P4
- bimodal by model: tax-inclusive bit Fable 33/40 finals and Astra 0/40 (gst P3)

**What killed it**

- one extra cue on every line: gst 1.5.0/1.6.0 P3 10/15 to 1/5 and 0/5 ('a single figure on each line', a second 'nothing to add on' note)
- student P3 tax-exclusive quote: 0/15 F, 0/10 A

**Trace signals**

- the user corrects a total because a price already included tax or covered two items
- receipts or tallies that disagree with list prices
- retail or workshop purchasing

**Domains**

- small retail and stationery
- workshop and maker BOMs
- trading-card and hobby budgets
- merchandising price matrices

**Example.** A bearing listed at one price per line is ordered one per piece, though the seller's own filed orders and the receiving tally show each line delivered two pieces.

### T1: Keying slip: a record filed under an ID it does not belong to

- **Status:** proven
- **Family:** record_reversal
- **Aimed at:** both
- **Clock trap:** no
- **Aliases:** ID.slip, keying slip, governing_vs_filing_key
- **Pooled misses:** Fable 742/1835 (40%), Astra 602/1490 (40%)
- **Loopholes:** L-record, L-join, L-union, L-threshold

A structured record (export row, log entry, filed note) carries an ID that is not its owner's. The default joins on the printed key and prices, counts or routes the record under that ID. The files make that impossible: two or more first-hand remarks from different parties describe the record's content, and only the true owner fits. Fable usually notices the conflict and keeps the record anyway; Astra joins silently. Measured here (build_evidence.py, 2026-10-15): final 10x2 pooled Fable 345/680, Astra 321/670; all stages Fable 742/1835, Astra 602/1490 (AMBIGUOUS/DEFECT units excluded); without unaudited >=90% units, final Fable 297/610, Astra 257/580. Audited GENUINE in 13 task(s). Status proven: audited GENUINE in 13 tasks; pooled final miss rate >= 0.2 with n >= 20 on fable, astra (unaudited >= 90% units excluded).

**Recipe**

- *record:* an official-looking keyed record (export row, dispatch line, inbox row) whose own content fits its printed key well enough to pass a casual check
- *anchors:* two or more by-the-way remarks from different parties, each in a document read for another purpose, that describe the record's content and fit only the true owner; at least one makes the printed owner impossible (a date, a location, a transfer)
- *withheld:* no sentence gives the key or record priority, and no sentence names the mechanism of the slip (no 'typo', no 'page-number slip'); the solver supplies the mechanism
- *dials:*
  - one by-the-way anchor per notch toward solvable (a witness of the event or a unique content match)
  - for Astra change the KIND of evidence, not the amount
  - never add a descriptor only the true owner has: it flips the bite to 0
- *conservation:* when the record could be credited to both IDs, grade both sides against a conserved total so the union answer loses
- *shields:* if a tempting rule exists, scope it away from the keyed variable explicitly

**Proof obligations (authoring/validate.py)**

- uniqueness: solve.py enumerates the candidate owners and exactly one fits every anchor
- ablation: dropping each anchor leaves the owner underdetermined or the default (executed)
- no-priority grep: no fixture sentence binds the printed key (audits withdrew psychopathy 2.1.0, ktp 2.1.0, cafe 2.1.0 for one)
- additivity: the keyed reading fails exactly this premise's checks (design_lint)
- blind reader before any dial when a model misses 5/5

**Hazards**

- a sentence that blesses the record makes it AMBIGUOUS: psychopathy 2.1.0 P4 ('the log decides'), ktp 2.1.0 P2 ('the class journal records each lesson'), cafe 2.1.0 P10 (clause 2.2 'drawings govern')
- two records that conflict symmetrically with nothing to decide between them: coloring 2.2.0 P2/P5/P8 AMBIGUOUS, fixed by one first-hand remark in 2.3.0
- a column whose provenance is unstated while a rule defers to it: probation 2.2.0 P4 (AMBIGUOUS, re-audit UNCLEAR)
- a later dated note that supports the printed label: feast 1.1.0 P3 AMBIGUOUS
- a graded label that exists in no file: line-colour 1.0.0 P2 DEFECT
- a 'credit both' reading left open when nothing makes the printed owner impossible: ats 1.1.0 and 1.6.0 P2, closed by an impossibility anchor in 1.7.0
- often flat: 10 of the 19 audited units in the 45-task pass were missed at least 90% by Fable (kibo P3, pathway P2, ats P2, suitcase P5)

**What killed it**

- one descriptor only the true owner has: cnc 2.4.0/2.5.0 P3 'still coiled, wire-tied' took 15/15 to 0/5 on both models
- a specific first-hand noun: coloring 2.4.0/2.5.0 'little crow' took Fable to 2/15, 4/15, 0/15
- a new note placed beside the anchor primes a destination check: gst 1.5.0 P1 11/15 to 0/5 with byte-identical premise text
- one origin remark: gst 1.5.0 P4 'lorry up from Hubballi' 13/15 to 0/5
- kibo 2.8.0 P3 dial (hand-typed name route) overshot to 0/5 on both

**Trace signals**

- the user corrects which file, row, ID or person an item belongs to
- two lists keyed by IDs that are easy to transpose (adjacent register lines, consecutive slide or order numbers)
- a record built by someone other than the people who saw the event (data entry from paper slips, an export of hand-typed names)

**Domains**

- school registers and option slips
- procurement and goods received
- applicant tracking

- small-business bookkeeping
