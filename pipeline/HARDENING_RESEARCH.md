# Capability-hard construction

Research reviewed on October 1, 2026. These are design hypotheses, not a promise
that either exact calibration model fails. The current completed screens are
mostly saturated; existing traps have not established extreme difficulty.

## Changes to the authoring loop

`author/challenge.py` supplies initial authoring and manual hardening with
source-specific construction diagnostics. Local draft checks expose the same
non-blocking profile. It describes causal dependencies, candidate/criterion
coverage, successful declared tool baselines and retained per-model aggregate
scores. It never consumes solver answers or creates a validity rejection.

The private near-miss lattice generates a full-match outline and an alternative
missing each distinct criterion. These are abstract planning aids, not fixture
evidence, reference answers or executed controls. Authors must populate coherent
public witnesses, independently derive answers, and run the existing controls.

The private `plan_challenge` author tool additionally constructs explicitly
selected multi-criterion misses, promotion/invariance control outlines and the
causal recomputation scope of a revision. It separates unaffected decisions
from descendants that need rechecking; a rechecked answer need not change.
The same tool is available to initial authors and manual hardening authors.
It does not write fixtures, choose reference answers, validate semantic truth,
predict pass@1, impose a trap-count quota or consume an additional allowance.

Stretch objectives remain substantive, publicly required and independently
testable. Construction diagnostics report the current 1–4 stretch band, available
slots, fully passing stretch checks and recipes linked to existing declared
professional mechanisms. The author planning tool reads the same staged-draft
profile without retransmitting a large specification. When the band is full,
strengthen existing eligible objectives instead of padding the rubric. Labels
and increased weight do not establish difficulty; each proposed check still
needs source support, legitimate alternatives, coherent interventions, wrong
policy controls, independent review and native measurement. Minimum-change
optimality cannot be silently imposed on an ordinary repair request.

The hardening brief no longer caps a family at two traps or prohibits additional
coherent evidence. Source-fidelity, privacy and scenario-byte limits remain.
Public equivalence, numeric tolerances and field definitions cannot be tightened
merely to manufacture failures. Three lineage attempts remain the maximum;
neither a failed repair nor a new source-copy restores spent attempts.

Transport interruptions preserve any durable author draft and its exact remaining
response budget. A restart verifies the source identity, conversation, workspace
and repair findings before resuming the same target. Incomplete boundaries require
inspection, never deletion or an automatic fresh authoring budget. An untouched
copy with no author session can still be removed safely.

## Research-backed hypotheses

- **Matter-room portfolio synthesis:** [Harvey LAB](https://github.com/harveyai/harvey-labs)
  supplies realistic multi-document professional work, but its low all-pass
  scores do not transfer automatically to deterministic tasks. The private
  `plan_portfolio` tool groups related fields, scopes source controls, records
  shared-premise cascades and requires sustained wrong-policy counterexamples.
  [LAB_PORTFOLIO_RESEARCH.md](LAB_PORTFOLIO_RESEARCH.md) records the pinned source
  inspection, verified exact-model scores, transfer recipes and statistical limits.
  No judge, benchmark answer key, extra objective quota or allowance is imported.

- **Global planning:** [DeepPlanning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.18137) separates
  information gathering, local constraints and global optimization. Construct
  interacting feasible allocations where greedy individual choices lose, rather
  than adding independent arithmetic fields. Accept all legitimate solutions.
- **Compositional state:** [tau2-bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07982) uses
  executable components and analyzes longer action sequences and dependencies.
  Use real prerequisite/state transitions and selective revision cascades, not
  a required private sequence or artificial grader gating. Its older-model
  results are not pass-rate predictions for the models in this campaign.
- **Semantic retrieval:** [NoLiMa](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05167) studies
  retrieval without direct literal matching and controlled distractors. Use
  professionally equivalent wording and plausible criterion-specific negatives;
  preserve accessible discriminators and all synonymous valid answers.
- **Inverse identification:** [BrowseComp](https://openai.com/index/browsecomp/)
  constructs difficult search questions with comparatively simple verification.
  Ask which item or combination satisfies distributed evidence, not for a
  winner already identified in a summary. Offline tasks retain all required
  evidence locally; they do not depend on a disappearing external page.
- **Benchmark validity:** the [tau benchmark repository](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench)
  explicitly versions task fixes and grading changes. Preserve old scores when
  rules change; validate and measure a distinct artifact instead of silently
  reinterpreting past model failures.
- **Offline search applicability:** [BrowseComp-Plus](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.1023/)
  verifies sufficient evidence in a fixed corpus and mines topical hard negatives.
  This is a closer fit to our local fixtures than unrestricted web-search scores.
  Keep a complete evidence set accessible and distinguish retrieval quality from
  end-to-end agent performance; do not manufacture hardness by removing tools.
- **Reasoning before retrieval:** [BRIGHT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.12883)
  targets queries where literal or semantic resemblance is insufficient without
  reasoning about the underlying problem. Near-misses should require actual
  applicability judgments, not simply different names or bigger files.
- **Stateful applicability:** [ToolSandbox](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.04682)
  evaluates state dependencies and milestones across valid alternative
  trajectories. Use this only where the environment actually implements shared
  state; an offline worksheet is not an interactive stateful benchmark.

## Applying the hypotheses to this ten-task cohort

These are proposed redesigns. Each needs source support and independent review
before it can be benchmarked; they are not claims about the live frozen drafts.

| Task | Substantive construction to consider | Essential validity control |
|---|---|---|
| Release audit | Shared implementing artifacts, revision-cut membership, operational dependencies and a jointly feasible remediation plan | Distinguish committed versus projected dates publicly; recutting affects only genuine descendants |
| Medical content | Competing routine, catch-up and risk-scope statements feeding a shared minimum coverage allocation | Exercise each schedule category independently; published fictional rules, not unstated clinical knowledge |
| Bibliography | Identify a work through edition, translation, authorship and evidence authority together | Promote each important partial match; accept legitimate equivalents and unresolved identity outcomes |
| History | Reconcile chapter, folio, OCR uncertainty and later scope authorization without cross-scope contamination | Preserve source uncertainty; separately intervene on scope and reading, not invented historical facts |
| Trading | Event-time and effective-time amendments, ledger classifications, eligible profit and coupled risk limits | Coherent chronological controls; deposits/fees/open positions handled under explicit public rules |
| UX | Global consent, retention and dependency feasibility across components and scoped revisions | A plausible local fix must leave a real violated constraint; accept every compliant alternative design |
| SME advisory | Joint funding eligibility, obligations and resource feasibility, not independent recommendations | Source-supported conditions and all acceptable solutions; do not silently assume eligibility |
| Small-business planning | Cash timing, capacities and competing commitments make locally attractive selections infeasible | Independently verified feasible plan and genuine greedy counterexample; public uncertainty and tie rules |
| Learning assessment | Mutually constrained coverage, prerequisite validity and a selective assessment revision | Promotion/invariance controls, complete affected-set recomputation and preservation of valid existing work |
| Market research | Entity/metric/time/population reconciliation feeding a coherent decision under uncertainty | Genuine scope controls, no inference from missing data unless public policy permits it |

## What counts as success

The campaign goal remains official **and** conservative capability-adjusted
pass@1 <= 0.2 on each exact model, with five development episodes followed by
ten independent qualification episodes. Medical additionally measures both
steps separately. An observed 0/5 or 2/10 is a sample result, not proof that the
population pass probability is below 0.2. No new sequential sampling scheme or
extra hardening allowance is authorized by these construction aids.

Reference/control success proves particular validity properties, not frontier
incapability. Every all-fail check still needs attribution evidence. A one-field
failure with 98% mean reward cannot be called broad extreme difficulty without
checking that field's semantics. Model safeguards or infrastructure failures
are excluded from capability-hardness claims.

Existing author/solver processes keep their pinned pipeline snapshots. To use
this implementation, freeze a new controller at a settled boundary and retain
its code, task identities, author history, attempts and scores. Do not hot-patch
the active batch or adopt old measurements under a changed policy identity.
